Frequently Asked Question!

1. What products does HM Grains export?

We specialize in Himalayan salt (edible, industrial, and decorative). Our product range includes fine crystal salt, coarse salt, salt blocks, powder salt, for international markets.

2. What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ is 500–1000 kg per product type, depending on the product. For bulk container shipments, we also offer flexible arrangements.

3. Do you provide certifications with your products?

Yes. All shipments come with the required export and health certifications. On request, we can also provide ISO, HACCP, FDA, and Organic certificates for specific product categories.

4. Can you handle custom packaging and private labeling?

Absolutely. We provide bulk, retail-ready, and private label packaging to match the branding and requirements of your market.

 

5. Which markets do you currently serve?

We export primarily to Europe, the Middle East, and North America, but we are open to building partnerships worldwide.

6. How do you ensure product quality?

Every batch is inspected and tested for purity, mineral content, and consistency. We work with certified labs and follow strict quality control at every stage.

7. What are your payment terms?

We usually work with bank transfers (TT) or LC at sight for large orders. Payment terms can be discussed based on the order size and client profile.

8. How long does shipping take?

Once the order is confirmed, typical shipping times are:

  • Europe: 20–30 days

  • Middle East: 10–15 days

  • North America: 30–40 days
    Shipping time may vary depending on port schedules and logistics.

9. Do you provide samples before bulk orders?

Yes, we can arrange paid samples that can be shipped internationally. The sample cost is deductible from your bulk order.
